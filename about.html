My Story

Passion Through
The Lens

My name is Amaru/Paul, based in Embakasi, Nairobi, Kenya — a Computer Science student at the University of Nairobi with a deep passion for the art of photography.

Inspired by international photographers through TV shows, social media and YouTube, photography became one of my greatest talents — a medium I use not just professionally, but as a form of artistic expression.

My work spans aesthetic, portrait, creativity and lifestyle — each frame a story waiting to be told. I believe every photo should stir an emotion, capture a moment, or tell a story that words cannot.

My main aim of being a photographer is not only to become a professional in it but also doing it as an artistical work — blending science and creativity in every shot.
