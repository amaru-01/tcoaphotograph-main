About
Amaru

My name is Amaru/Paul, based in Embakasi, Nairobi, Kenya — a Computer Science student at the University of Nairobi with a deep passion for the art of photography.

Inspired by international photographers through TV shows, social media and YouTube, photography became one of my greatest talents — a medium I use not just professionally, but as a form of artistic expression.

My work spans aesthetic, portrait, creativity and lifestyle — each frame a story waiting to be told.
